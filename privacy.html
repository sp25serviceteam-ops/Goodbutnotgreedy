Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 August 2026

Cafe Care Package ("we", "us", "our") builds free tools to help cafes and wholesalers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email lukas@kiness.com.au.

Who this covers

  • Cafe and wholesaler owners who create an account with us.
  • Customers who reserve items through a cafe's page.

What we collect

  • Account holders: your name and email (via Google sign-in or an email and password), plus the cafe details you enter, such as your shop name, pickup times, payment details and logo.
  • Customers: the name and phone number you provide when reserving a bag, and any payment screenshot you choose to upload.
  • Technical: basic data needed to keep the service running and secure.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and basic profile info from Google so we can create and secure your account. We never see or store your Google password. We only request the minimum permissions needed to sign you in.

How we use your information

  • To run your dashboard, display your listings, and process customer reservations.
  • To let customers contact you and collect their order.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored securely with our infrastructure providers, Supabase (database and authentication) and Netlify (hosting). We share data with these providers only as needed to run the service.

Your choices

You can edit or remove your listings at any time from your dashboard. To delete your account or request a copy of your data, email lukas@kiness.com.au and we'll sort it out.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email lukas@kiness.com.au.
