Questions

The things you should ask before connecting anything.

Do cafes need an app or account?

No. Each cafe receives its own private ordering link and orders in the browser. There is no app to install or customer password to remember.

Does Care Pack replace Xero?

No. Care Pack structures the cafe order and creates a draft against the matched Xero contact. Coded products use the Xero item master for pricing.

Can different cafes see different products?

Yes. You can manage customer-specific catalogue visibility, coffee item codes, minimum quantities and delivery information.

What happens if Xero refuses an invoice?

The order and invoice outcome remain visible in the dashboard so the problem can be investigated rather than silently disappearing.

Is the 50-account number a product limit?

No. It only determines whether the initial customer migration is included. Customers added after onboarding do not change the weekly price.

Does Care Pack take a cut of my sales?

No. Your orders and invoices carry no Care Pack percentage, however many you do. Collecting payment from your cafes is a separate optional service with its own price, and we tell you exactly what it costs before you switch it on. Your payment provider bills you directly for processing; we take no part of that and do not mark it up.
