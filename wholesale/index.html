How it works

Open portal, send order, invoices get paid.

Three steps. Your cafe does the first two.

01 / ORDER

The cafe orders.

They open their own link and see the products, minimums and delivery notes you set for them.

02 / CONFIRM

They check it over.

Nothing goes through empty, and they can’t order something you’ve marked sold out.

03 / XERO

It lands in Xero, you get paid.

Straight onto the right contact, priced from your item codes. No double handling, and with direct debit on, no chasing either.
