Wholesale Care Package

Wholesale orders shouldn't live in your text messages.

I ran wholesale for a coffee roaster in Melbourne. Just under 200 cafes. They ordered by text, by phone, by whatever was handy, and every one of those orders got retyped into Xero by hand. Usually by me, usually at night.

So I built this portal. It still runs that roaster's whole book today, and now I'm opening it up to a handful of other roasters.

Wholesale ordering
Proven on just under 200 cafes' worth of daily orders. Looking for a few roasters to try it next.
  • ✓Each cafe gets one link. It opens with your name and logo on it, they tap what they need, done. Saved to a phone's home screen it opens like an app, so nobody goes digging through old texts to find it.
  • ✓Set up from your Xero. Connect it and we read the products, customers and per-cafe codes you already have in there. You confirm them. You don't retype a catalogue you already own once.
  • ✓Every order lands in your Xero as a draft. You look it over and approve it like any other invoice. We never send anything to your customers.
  • ✓Payments stay wherever they are now. Pinch, Stripe, direct debit through whoever you already use. We take nothing, because you shouldn't be paying anyone a cut of sales you worked your arse off for. If you want cafes set up on direct debit before they can order, that's a switch you can flick.
  • ✓This isn't a marketplace. Your cafes are never listed next to another roaster.
