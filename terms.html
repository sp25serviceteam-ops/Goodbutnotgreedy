What the service is

Care Pack is two things under one roof.

  • Free tools for cafes. Including the tool for listing and selling surplus food. These are provided free of charge and "as is".
  • Care Pack wholesale ordering. A paid subscription for roasters and wholesalers: private ordering links for your customers, a dashboard, and draft invoices written into your own Xero.

The service connects you with your own customers. It is not a marketplace, and we do not advertise competing suppliers to the customers you bring.

What wholesale costs

  • $50 per week for the wholesale subscription. The price does not change with the number of cafes you serve, the number of orders they place, or the value of what you sell.
  • A one-off $500 if we migrate more than 50 existing accounts for you, to cover that work.
  • Collecting payment from your cafes is a separate optional service. It is off unless you switch it on. When it is on we charge 0.25% of each payment, capped at $5, charged separately from the $50. That is the only part of the service where what we charge is calculated on a transaction. Your payment processor bills you separately for its own processing fees; we take no part of those and do not mark them up.

Outside of that optional payment service, we do not take a percentage of your sales.
